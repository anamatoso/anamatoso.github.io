Well, after my post on the Arc Browser, I thought I had found my browser. However, when they stopped upgrades for my old 2015 Macbook Pro, I had enough and started experimenting with other browsers. 
<br /><br />
<p align="justify">However, at the time I had heard of another browser that was basically Arc but firefox based: Zen browser (<a href="https://zen-browser.app/">https://zen-browser.app/</a>). I experimented it but found it too buggy and so the one that stuck was the one I already used at work: Firefox. This was around the beginning of 2025.</p>

<p align="justify">owever, last month I decided to give t another try, and was very surprised with what I saw, and in my personal computer I completely changed to Zen browser. From time to time, i still have some issues with the sidebar when swipping between spaces, but I find that they are easily fixed and attribute the problem to my fast swipping.</p>

<p align="justify">I don't have much more to say about it, other that it does not have the AI features that Arc was pushing but I only used the clean tabs one, which I don't miss. All the other things I said I liked in the Arc browser post are present in Zen browser. And basically all the things I didn't like are solved.</p>
<br />
As such, and as of now, I think that Zen browser is at least worth the try.
